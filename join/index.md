# Work with me

I am always interested in new collaborations on rapid adaptation, physiological and population genomics, epigenetics, and resilience to environmental change, from experimental systems like copepods to species of conservation and management concern.

## Collaborators

If you are working on related questions or have a system or dataset where genomic, epigenomic, or physiological approaches could help, I would be happy to talk. I have ongoing collaborations across NOAA Fisheries, universities, and research institutes in the US and Europe.

## Students

In my current position at NOAA, I am not able to directly host graduate students. However, I am open to co-advising, serving on thesis committees, and collaborating on projects with students based elsewhere. If this sounds like you, please reach out with a short description of your interests and background.

## Postdocs

I periodically have funding to support postdocs, and  typically hire these positions via the University of Miami Cooperative Institute for Marine & Atmospheric Studies ([CIMAS](https://cimas.earth.miami.edu/)). 

Regardless of funding, I welcome inquiries from candidates interested in developing fellowship proposals together and am generally interested in supporting and collaborating with outside postdocs however I can.

Still interested? Please [get in touch](https://brennan-research.github.io/contact/)!
